# 16: Year end: Promotion and Closing

**What to build:** The Admin promotes Classes into the next Academic Year, handles Repeating and Leaving Students, then Closes the old year so it becomes read-only.

**Blocked by:** 06 (Enrollment), 07 (Lessons), 08 (Assessments and marks grid), 13 (Exam timetable)

- [x] The Admin Closes an Academic Year after Promotion
